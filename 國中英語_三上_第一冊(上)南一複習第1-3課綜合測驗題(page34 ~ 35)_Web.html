A Mother’s Love Across Borders: Facing Challenges in Taiwan

In Taiwan, many children are raised by foreign mothers. These mothers are often called foreign spouses. For most of them, raising a child in a new country is not an easy job. One of the problems is language. Many foreign mothers do not speak Chinese well, so it’s hard for them to help their kids with homework. Their children may also feel shy when using their mother’s language. This would make them miss the chance to learn two cultures. Another problem is that some foreign spouses come from poor families, so they cannot pay for more classes for their children. Also, if they don’t have much education, they may not know how to help their children with their learning.

Not having enough family time is another problem. A woman from Vietnam said, “I have to work long hours. After a tiring day, I feel too exhausted to take care of my children and their homework.” Worst of all, discrimination against these foreign mothers and their children is still common. Sometimes, these kids face bullying at school.

It is clear that foreign spouses and their kids must deal with these challenges. If we can help them more, they can have a better life here.

自修 p.35 跨域時事 ｜ 對應第 9～15、36～40、46～50 題
Rainwater Harvesting

Did you know that Taiwan is facing water stress? This means that as people living on this island, we should try to save water. Because Taiwan is such a small island, building a new reservoir is very difficult. There are other ways to make the best use of the water we get.

Rainwater harvesting is a good idea that some buildings in Taiwan use to save water. We do get a lot of rain here, but most of it goes back into the sea without being used. If we save the rain and use it later, this will fix a lot of problems.

Buildings with rainwater harvesting work like this: the rain will run down the roof and into the gutter, where it will go under the ground into the main tank. Before flowing into the main tank, the water will go through a filter, which will make sure that the water kept is clean. This way, we can keep the water from the rain under the house! The water can be used to water plants, wash cars, and more, so it will also help save some money.

If this sounds good to you, call us for more information!

【RAINWATER HARVESTING 系統流程圖解】
Rain (雨水) ➔ Roof (屋頂) ➔ Gutter (排水溝) ➔ Filter (過濾器) ➔ Underground Main Tank (地下主儲水槽) ➔ Garden watering (澆花) / Clothes washing (洗衣) / Toilet flushing (沖馬桶) / Car washing (洗車)
