閱讀理解（一）｜對應第 41～45 題

Whose Moms Are Nurses? — A Special Community

In Sunshine Junior High School, Tina and Nora are best friends and classmates. They walk to school together every morning. Many people think they are sisters because they look very similar, and their moms both work at Hope Hospital.

Actually, Tina and Nora are not from the same family. Tina's family name is Brown, and Nora's family name is White. Tina's and Nora's moms are nurses at the same hospital, and they are good friends, too! Every Friday evening, the two families meet at Tina's place. The mothers talk about their work in the living room, while Tina and Nora do their homework at the table in the dining room. Sometimes, Tina's uncle, Dr. Brown, joins them for dinner. He is a famous writer and doctor. The children always listen to his interesting stories carefully.
