【Text B】Four Kinds of Pressure on Teenagers
Teenagers today face four main kinds of pressure: (1) Peer pressure—caring too much about how friends see them, which may lead to bad behavior like smoking; (2) Pressure of being successful at school—worrying that bad grades will be judged by classmates or parents; (3) Parental pressure—parents pushing children to do well in every part of life, affecting how children see themselves; and (4) Pressure from technology—FOMO (a fear of missing out), making people constantly check their cellphones so they don't miss any messages or information.

A Mother's Love Across Borders & Rainwater Harvesting

【Reading 1】A Mother's Love Across Borders: Facing Challenges in Taiwan
In Taiwan, many children are raised by foreign mothers, often called foreign spouses. Raising a child in a new country brings several challenges. First, many foreign mothers do not speak Chinese well, making it hard to help their kids with homework, while their children may feel shy using their mother's language and miss the chance to learn two cultures. Second, some foreign spouses come from poor families or lack education, so they cannot pay for extra classes or guide their kids' learning. Third, long working hours leave them too exhausted to care for their children's schoolwork. Worst of all, discrimination against foreign mothers and bullying at school still happen. If we offer more help, they can have a better life here.

【Reading 2】Rainwater Harvesting
Taiwan is facing water stress, and building a new reservoir on this small island is very difficult. Rainwater harvesting is a smart way some buildings use to save water. Although Taiwan gets plenty of rain, most of it flows back into the sea unused. In a rainwater harvesting system, rain runs down the roof into the gutter, passes through a filter to become clean, and is stored under the ground in the main tank. This stored water can be used for watering plants, washing cars, washing clothes, and flushing toilets, helping people save both water and money.
