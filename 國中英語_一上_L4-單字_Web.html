(On the sidewalk)
Ha-Joon: BLACKPINK is great! I can listen to their songs again and again!
Ann: Same here. Hey! Check out their Instagram. We can watch their live stream.
Rita: Cool! I can't wait!
Ann: Come on, let's cross the road.
Rita: Ha-Joon, watch out! Are you OK?
Ha-Joon: I'm fine, thanks. Sorry, I'm a real phubber.
Rita: Ha-Joon, don't look down at your phone all the time.
Ha-Joon: You're right. Guess what? In South Korea, some traffic lights are on the ground now. See? "How you like that?"
Ann & Rita: That's a great idea!
知識補充｜交通安全與詞彙解析
Ground Traffic Lights & Road Users
In South Korea, some traffic lights are on the ground to warn phubbers (smartphone users who look down at their phones all the time) when they cross the road or crosswalk. Road users can see the red, yellow, and green lights clearly on the ground without looking up. Remember: never run a red light, and always watch out for oncoming cars!
