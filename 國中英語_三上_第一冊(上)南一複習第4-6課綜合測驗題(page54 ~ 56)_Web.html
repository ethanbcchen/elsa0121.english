After School: Staying Up Late for a Science Report

Jason: Hey, Linda. You look tired today. Are you OK?

Linda: No. I stayed up last night. I worked on my science report until 2 a.m. I can't even keep my eyes open now. I feel so sleepy.

Jason: Well, I can tell that. You should hit the sack earlier tonight.

Linda: I would like to, but the deadline is coming soon. I didn't think it would take so long to find the information I need.

Jason: I know how that feels. But getting enough sleep is sometimes more important. If you don't sleep early, you'll feel tired and might make mistakes the next day.

Linda: I agree. I almost fell asleep in each class today. I kept telling myself, "I need to sleep earlier and not stay up late again."

Jason: That's right! Don't forget what you said. Go home and sleep before 10:00.

Linda: Alright, I'll try my best. Maybe I'll just do a little more reading and then go to bed.

Jason: Good idea! Hope you feel better. See you tomorrow!

Linda: See you!

文本二（P.55 閱讀素養）｜ 對應第 6～10、33～35 題

Germany's Deposit Revolution

What do you do with empty bottles? Do you throw them away? No. You can get some money back right now if you recycle them in Germany.

Germany has the deposit system. The deposit system, in simple terms, is when a customer pays a small fee to the store when they buy something in recyclable packaging. Then after the customer uses the product, the packaging is returned to the store or a recycling center, and the deposit is returned when the packaging is returned to the recycling center.

So, after you drink up bottled water, you can take the bottle back to the supermarket and get your deposit back by putting it in a bottle recycling machine. The bottle recycling machine will give you a receipt. You can get your money back or buy something else in the supermarket with the receipt.

With the deposit system, more and more people are willing to recycle empty bottles. Isn't the idea smart and easy for people to recycle plastic bottles?

核心字彙片語： deposit (n.) 押金 ｜ revolution (n.) 創舉；革新 ｜ recycle (v.) 回收 ｜ system (n.) 系統 ｜ term (n.) 詞彙 ｜ customer (n.) 消費者 ｜ fee (n.) 費用 ｜ receipt (n.) 收據 ｜ plastic (adj./n.) 塑膠 ｜ empty (adj.) 空的

Is AI Good or Bad?

Artificial intelligence, or AI, is causing some problems in Hollywood. It's not just about money, but also about making movies and TV shows. AI can create things without people. This has even led to actors and writers going on strike.

Some actors are really worried and angry. They are afraid of losing control over their looks in movies. New actors are also scared because AI might take away their jobs. Also, the actors accused film companies of wanting to use actors' pictures without asking or telling them. Writers are afraid AI might take their story credit. They think AI should just be a useful tool at most.

AI is already in many parts of making movies, like making old actors look young again. Hollywood actors and writers understand that, but they want people to think about the ways of using AI, such as "How should people use it?" or "Who should control it?" In Hollywood, it is already difficult for writers and actors to get credit. Adding AI makes it even harder. Both sides are trying to find a way to use AI without losing their rights and credit.

核心字彙片語： Artificial intelligence (n.) 人工智慧 ｜ create (v.) 創作 ｜ go on strike 罷工 ｜ control (n./v.) 控制 ｜ accuse ... of 指控 ｜ credit (n.) 功勞；署名權 ｜ existing (adj.) 現存的
