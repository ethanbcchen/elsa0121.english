Saturday Afternoon in the Park

It is three thirty on Saturday afternoon, and the weather is sunny and warm. Many people are enjoying their weekend in Yongkang Park. Near the big pond, two old men are playing chess under a tall tree, and several children are watching them quietly.

Between the playground and the flower garden, Amy and her younger brother, Leo, are flying a kite. Their dog, Lucky, is running happily around them. On the bench in front of the garden, their parents are sitting together. Mr. Lin is reading a newspaper, while Mrs. Lin is talking on her cellphone with her sister in Taipei. Everyone is having a wonderful time!
