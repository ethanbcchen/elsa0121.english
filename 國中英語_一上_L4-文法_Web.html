課文核心文本｜Smart Designs
Smart Designs（智慧設計）
A traffic light on the ground is not the only smart design for phubbers. In South Korea, smartphone users can download an app on their phones. It can warn them about the oncoming cars at crosswalks! Also, in the USA, phubbers can walk in special phone lanes now. That way, smartphone users are only in the way of other phubbers. Smart, right? Can you see smart designs like these in Taiwan, too?

HiMusic 免費音樂應用程式
Hank: Can I use your smartphone? I want to listen to Sunshine's new songs again.
Richard: Yes, but why can't you use your smartphone?
Hank: I   (31)   get on the Internet here.
Richard: What? Aren't Sunshine's songs on your smartphone?
Hank: No. In fact, I have no songs on my smartphone. I have no money to download any songs.
Richard:   (32)  , but I use HiMusic.
Hank: What's that?
Richard: It's a free app.   (33)  
Hank: Really? Is it safe? How can it be free?
Richard: Hmm, that's a good question. I have no idea, but all my friends use it. Come on,   (34)  , and you will like it.
Hank: OK. Thank you.
