克漏字題組 (一) ｜ 對應第 31～35 題
Room Makeover with an AI Tool（居家空間大改造）
Bella is at Scott's home. Scott's house is nice and quiet, but it is not really his style. Bella gives him a good idea: "What about a makeover? Let's get some ideas from RoomGPT." Scott asks, "What's that?" Bella tells him that it is a cool AI tool on her smartphone. First, (31) a photo of the room. Second, choose a room type, like the living room, the bathroom, or the bedroom. Next, choose a room theme. Scott looks (32) the themes on the phone and thinks they are really cool! However, Bella says, "Wait. A big makeover is expensive. Are you OK (33) that?" Scott laughs and says, "Ha! That's a good question." In fact, they can just paint the wall or put a new blanket (34) the bed. Let's (35) sad about the money and start small!
閱讀理解題組 (一) ｜ 對應第 41～45 題
Unique Homes and Beds Around the World（世界各地的獨特房屋與床鋪）
Is your bed big and soft, or is it small and hard? In fact, beds around the world are very different. Let's meet two new friends and see their special homes.

Jenny is an eleven-year-old girl. Her house is an igloo. It is made of ice and snow. Inside the igloo, there are no separate walls between rooms. This single space is her bedroom, kitchen, living room, and dining room, too. Where is her bed? It is beside the kitchen. It is a big and hard ice bed, covered with warm animal skins.

Jason is twenty-one years old. His house is inside a quiet cave. Where is his bed? It is next to the wooden table. It is a small and old blanket on the cave floor. Both Jenny and Jason love their special beds very much!
