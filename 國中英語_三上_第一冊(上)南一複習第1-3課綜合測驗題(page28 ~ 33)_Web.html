【自修重點文本 A】基礎字彙、家族與名詞單複數（p.28–p.29） 對應第 1–15 題

1. 核心詞彙與家庭成員：woman / women（女人）、man / men（男人）、person（人）、cousin（堂表兄弟姊妹）、classmate（同班同學）、daughter（女兒）、police officer（警察）、cook（廚師）、singer（歌手）、office worker（上班族）、writer（作家）、housewife（家庭主婦）、farmer（農夫）。

2. 形容詞與日常描述：hungry（飢餓的）、quiet（安靜的）、noisy（吵雜的）、useful（有用的）、clear（清楚的）、careful（小心的）、handsome（大方的；英俊的）、stray（流浪的）、dirty（髒的）。

3. 場所與生活物品：living room（客廳）、bedroom（臥室）、bathroom（浴室）、dining room（飯廳）、pencil box / pencil boxes（鉛筆盒）、toy bus / toy buses（玩具公車）、sofa（沙發）、knife（刀子）。

4. 關鍵文法與搭配：

  • 所有格辨析：Nora's and Tina's husbands（兩人各自的老公，後面加複數名詞）；Nora and Tina's room（兩人共有的房間，後面加單數名詞）。
  • 祈使句與連接詞：Join our club, and you can learn...（祈使句 + and + 陳述句，表示「只要……就會……」）；句首表命令或叮嚀用原形動詞 Remember to turn off the gas...。
  • 方位介系詞：above the table（在桌子正上方）；used to + V（過去習慣……）；No wonder（難怪）。

【自修閱讀文本 B】Sandra Green's Family Tree（p.29–p.30） 參考文本

Good afternoon, my name is Sandra Green. I am so happy to be here and share my family tree with you. Please take a look at this picture. This is my grandpa, Mark. He is a seventy-five-year-old farmer. He works for fun, not for money. He always shares his fruits and vegetables with people in town. He is happy on the farm. This nice lady is my grandma, Cathy. She used to be a housewife, but now she is a junior high school student at our night school. My dad, Richard, is a police officer. He is forty-five years old. My mother, Sandy, is a forty-year-old math teacher in our school. The little cute boy is my brother, Adam. He is eleven years old. By the way, we had a new family member last month. Her name is Apple. It was a stray dog. I found her on my way home, and she followed me home. Now, Adam and I like to play with her after school. We are teaching her to sit down, stand up, and shake hands. We are a happy family, and I love my family so much.

【自修閱讀文本 C】Gimei Islands International Fireworks Festival（p.31） 參考文本

Welcome to the Gimei Islands, the most beautiful islands in the Treasure Ocean. This year the festival starts on May 1st and continues until June 29th. Come to see the show!

Fireworks Schedule & Map Notes:
• Qiqi Island (Northeast): At 9 p.m., every Tuesday. With music playing. (Mascot: Bee)
• Gi-ma Island (North): At 9 p.m., every Monday and Saturday. With Drone shows. (Mascot: Goat)
• Mia-zan Island (Northwest): At 8 p.m., every Friday. With Drone shows. (Mascot: Sheep)
• Au-zi Island (Center): At 9 p.m., every Sunday and Monday. With Drone shows. (Mascot: Rabbit)
• Mei-an Island (Southwest): At 8 p.m., every Thursday. With music playing. (Mascot: Elephant)
• Ji-an Island (Far Southwest): Mascot: Butterfly.

Special events:
1. There will be an opening show on Au-zi Island on May 1st. Visitors will see a 20-minute fireworks show and a drone show as well.
2. Visitors can collect more than four stamps of mascots on a card to get one free special present.

【自修閱讀文本 D】Teenagers' Pressure & FOMO（p.33） 參考文本

Teenagers today have many different kinds of pressure.

Peer pressure: Teenagers care about how their friends see them, and this might lead to peer pressure, which may also cause bad or dangerous behavior, like smoking. Teenagers often make poor decisions because of such pressure.

Pressure of being successful at school: The stress of being successful at school can be challenging for teenagers. Many worry that a bad school performance will lead to being judged by their classmates or parents. Even high-performing students feel stressed about getting into the best schools.

Parental pressure: The pressure parents put on their children to do well in every part of their life can be bad for teenagers. What the parents say, either good or bad, can affect how a child sees themselves.

Pressure from technology: With the fast growth in technology, social media caused some serious problems. FOMO, a fear of missing out, is one of the serious problems: some people are always checking their cellphones because they are afraid that they might miss any information or messages.
